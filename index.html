SCIENTIFIC MODELPhysical, approximate, and explicitly fictional layers

Authoritative units: SI meters, kilograms, seconds in Float64 arrays.

Major gravity: mutual Newtonian gravity with G = 6.67430×10⁻¹¹ m³·kg⁻¹·s⁻² and velocity-Verlet integration.

Seeded systems: deterministic near-Keplerian eccentric planet and moon initial conditions, shifted into a barycentric rest frame. They are plausible sandbox systems, not claims of astrophysical formation history.

Trajectory path: an N-body forward integration of the current massive system plus the ship/projectile. Future thrust and minor-particle gravity are excluded.

Scanner orbit: osculating two-body elements around the selected target. Other-body perturbations are intentionally ignored in those displayed elements.

Minor field: 1k–20k typed-array background test particles feel major gravity but intentionally contribute no gravity.

Particle experiments: Gravity Cloud uses SI Float64 test particles under all major Newtonian gravity sources. Particle Life and Species Forces use a uniform spatial hash so local neighbor searches avoid all-pairs O(N²) work; their life/attraction rules are explicitly artificial. Experiment fields are session-local in v0.1.4 and cap warp at 60× for numerical resolution. Observation cameras are non-physical viewpoints; they do not alter particle or spacecraft state.

Ship: FLIGHT thrust is 20 m/s²; CRUISE thrust is 120 m/s² experimental propulsion. BRAKE applies bounded physical thrust opposite inertial velocity. APPROACH uses a propulsion-safe target stand-off, brakes into CAPTURE, then remains in thrust-powered HOLD until the pilot takes over. MATCH uses bounded thrust; none of these modes teleport or delete velocity.

Impacts: finite-radius contact and momentum/energy telemetry are physical. Low-energy collisions bounce/merge using simplified material restitution; energetic impacts use documented crater scaling and heuristic fragment partitioning. Large fragments remain real gravity sources; dense ejecta/fire is visual-only. This is not a hydrocode.

Cosmic phenomena: physical comet nuclei are live Newtonian gravity sources on high-eccentricity orbits. Asteroid/debris belts and planetary rings use seeded GPU-friendly point-population proxies tied to physical anchor bodies; their individual visible points do not source gravity or collide. Distant colored nebular haze is explicitly visual background.

Compact objects: black holes use Schwarzschild-radius metadata plus live Newtonian gravity, with a dense real-time accretion-disk/photon-ring/jet visual proxy. Neutron stars/pulsars use live Newtonian mass/radius plus visual magnetosphere and sweep-beam proxies. APPROACH computes propulsion-safe stand-off, strong gravity reduces integration steps, and model guards pause before near-horizon/near-surface or >10% c states are presented as adequate Newtonian science. Full GR ray tracing, radiation transport and plasma MHD remain future work.
