Gravity anomaly

Near a black hole, v0.1.0.1 uses Newtonian gravity only. Relativistic trajectories and gravitational lensing are future modules.

SCIENTIFIC MODELWhat is real, approximate, or experimental

Authoritative units: SI meters, kilograms, seconds in Float64 arrays.

Gravity: Newton's law using G = 6.67430×10⁻¹¹ m³·kg⁻¹·s⁻².

Major-body integration: velocity Verlet. Massive bodies mutually attract each other.

Minor field: 1k–20k test particles feel major-body gravity but intentionally contribute no gravity in this foundation build.

Floating origin: the renderer stays near the spacecraft while physics coordinates remain astronomical-scale Float64 values.

Ship DAMP: intentionally fictional inertial damping for navigation. THRUST is a 20 m/s² experimental drive.

Collisions: geometric contact and physical impact energy are detected. Deformation, cratering, debris and fluid response are not yet implemented.
